## La Mola Recovery — Infrared sauna (from October 2026)
- The only CrossFit box in Terrassa with an infrared sauna

## Civil service exam candidates (firefighters)
- For candidates registered for Bombers de la Generalitat or Bombers de Barcelona (SPEIS) exams
- Price: €80/month VAT included. No joining fee on first sign-up and no minimum term. Groups of more than 4 people: 10% off the first month
- Includes: programming specific to the physical tests; 3 coached classes a week (Monday, Wednesday and Friday 12:30–13:30: one wildland fire session, one structural fire session and one general conditioning session); 3 open training sessions a week (slots: Monday, Wednesday and Friday 10:30–15:30; Tuesday and Thursday 11:30–15:30) with coaches on the floor; candidates' WhatsApp group; monthly timed mock test of the full circuit; 1 infrared sauna session (15 min) a month
- Unlimited sauna (La Mola Recovery add-on): €9.90/month
- Coached classes and open sessions booked through the Aimharder app
- Bombers de la Generalitat 2026 (call 81/26, Resolution ISP/3218/2026): the two fire simulations are worked on at the box. The water rescue is prepared in a pool, not at the box
- Circuit equipment at the box: sled and drag lane, wall balls, boxes, dummy drag, balance beam and space for the running work
- Day pass €15; day pass + sauna €25
- Proof of exam registration required
- More info: https://crossfitlamola.com/en/opositors
